# SimLife day: payday, an upcoming bill, and a life event

## Suggested 45-minute class

1. **Opening (5 minutes):** Have students check their current checking balance and unpaid bills. Ask what they can afford before payday.
2. **Payday (5 minutes):** In Teacher → Banking, select the period and students, then use **Send paychecks** with each student's assigned pay. Preview the batch before posting. Students refresh their dashboard and decide what portion to keep in checking, save, or move to investing.
3. **Upcoming bill (5 minutes):** Announce a bill that will be sent later in the week. State both the planned release day and exact due date. On release day, use **Send bills**, choosing each student's rent or car payment or one flat amount. The app issues bills immediately when the teacher presses Send; it does not automatically release a bill on a future date. Bills remain unpaid until students pay them from checking.
4. **Life event wheel (15 minutes):** In the same Banking view, choose one student and spin. The server picks one of ten equally likely results and applies it to that student's account. Each student gets one result per New York calendar day. For a quick whole-class run, have students watch the projected spin for themselves or their table group.
5. **Decision window (10 minutes):** Students respond to the event: pay a bill, move money to savings, adjust spending plans, or explain how changed future pay affects the next week. A one-sentence response is enough: “My balance changed because __, so I will __.”
6. **Wrap-up (5 minutes):** Ask which students needed cash reserves and which outcomes changed only future income. This makes the distinction between a one-time expense, a bill, and a paycheck change visible.

Suggested announcement: “A new **[bill name]** will arrive on **[release date]**. It must be paid by **[due date]** from checking. Review your balance after payday and plan what you will keep available.”

## Wheel outcomes

| Result | Direct SimLife effect |
| --- | --- |
| Small inheritance | $200 deposit to checking |
| Tax refund | $100 deposit to checking |
| Weekend side gig | $75 deposit to checking |
| Unexpected rebate | $50 deposit to checking |
| Flat tire | $60 bill, due in 7 days |
| Phone repair | $90 bill, due in 7 days |
| Urgent care copay | $75 bill, due in 7 days |
| Parking ticket | $40 bill, due in 7 days |
| Promotion | Job title changes; future pay rises 10% |
| Laid off, then rehired | Job immediately changes to **Cashier at Arby's**; future pay becomes 85% of prior pay, capped at $400 per paycheck |

The expense outcomes create bills; they do not silently remove checking cash. Students must pay them. Deposit outcomes appear in checking history. Job outcomes change the assigned pay used by the next class paycheck batch. The wheel requires a job and positive assigned pay so all ten results are meaningful. A student cannot reroll on the same calendar day; retrying a request returns the existing result.

## Teacher checklist

- Confirm every selected student has a job and paycheck before spinning.
- Preview each payday batch and bill batch before posting.
- Announce a specific bill release day and due date. Send the bill manually on its release day.
- Let students refresh their dashboard to see the life event and their updated bank or job information.
